// 01 · about

    

      

        I am an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at
        Tsinghua University.
        Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from
        Dalian University of Technology.
      

      

        My research focuses on building Universal Digital Agents (UDAs), with an emphasis on
        Computer-Use Agents. I study how agents can understand and operate across digital environments,
        including graphical interfaces, terminals, and code. I am particularly interested in
        long-horizon reasoning, post-training, and Agentic RL for UDAs.
      

      

        I aim to make computer use a core capability of frontier models and to bring UDAs into
        everyday life. Computers serve as the core medium for these agents. By operating digital systems and connecting
        them with real-world tasks, they can improve accessibility, extend human capabilities, and create meaningful
        impact in the physical world. I believe UDAs represent an important path toward AGI.
      

      

        I am currently a research intern with the Qwen Team at Alibaba, where I am advised by
        Que Shen. Previously, I interned at Huawei Multimodal Lab and collaborated
        remotely with Microsoft Research Asia.
